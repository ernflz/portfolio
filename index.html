Studied architecture, with a focus on designing spaces that are both unique and intentional. This sensibility naturally extends to how those spaces are represented. Over time, this drew me toward maps as a spatial representation tool in their own right, and I'm now pursuing that interest further through an Erasmus Mundus M.Sc. in Cartography.

Currently focused on the relationship between maps and design processes: how the way we represent space shapes the way we plan, design, and understand it.

Thrive in collaborative environments, exchanging ideas across disciplines, and staying curious about the shifting technological scene.

Also a seasoned guitarist with teaching experience.
