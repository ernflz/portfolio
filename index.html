Projects

pressed/suppressed

A1 Print Map — 2026

Rather than representing press freedom through statistics alone, I layered the names of journalists killed or imprisoned across overlapping CMY color channels, producing a map that is illegible without applying color filters to separate the layers. The difficulty of reading it is deliberate: the information depicted is itself withheld or dangerous to access, and the map asks the viewer to make an effort proportional to that reality before it yields its content.

Carta Urla

Architectural Graduation Project — 2024

Standard urban analysis of Urla fragmented the town into isolated categorical layers: land use, infrastructure, heritage, losing the lived sense of a connected cultural landscape. In response, I adapted Olaus Magnus's Carta Marina (1539), a historical map that depicts land as a continuous, populated, and narratively rich territory, redrawing Urla in that pictorial style. The result reframes the town as an interconnected cultural plane rather than a set of planning categories, and became the starting point for my current interest in cartographic style as a design and political choice.
